Privacy policy

Applies to the Molkky Board Android app (com.yogistudio.molkkyboard), published by Yogi Studio. Last updated: 29 October 2026.

In short: Molkky Board has no account, no advertising, no trackers and no analytics. Yogi Studio collects no data about you: your games stay on your phones. When several phones play together, they talk directly to each other over the local network, encrypted, without going through the Internet or any server.

1. Who is responsible

Molkky Board is published by Yogi Studio, an individual developer based in France. Contact: yogistudiofr@gmail.com.

2. Data Yogi Studio collects

None. Yogi Studio runs no server for the app and receives no data from it: no account, no identifier, no location, no contacts, no usage statistics, no advertising identifier. The app contains no advertising, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting library (checked automatically against the Exodus Privacy tracker list at every build).

3. Data stored on your phone

To work, the app stores the following on your phone only:

  • the names or nicknames you enter for players and teams, their colors and emblems;
  • your games (every throw), from which scores, history, statistics, records and badges are computed;
  • a player photo, only if you choose to add one (taken with the camera or picked from your gallery; it is resized and re-encoded, without location or other metadata);
  • your settings (language, input mode, sounds, voice announcements…);
  • if the app crashes, a technical report (app version, Android version, phone model, error types; no game data and no identifier), kept until you decide what to do with it.

This data is excluded from Android cloud backups and device transfers. You can delete everything at any time in Settings › Data › “Delete all my data”, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Playing on several phones (“Play nearby”)

When the organizer shares a game, the other phones join by scanning a QR code. The phones then exchange data directly with each other, over the same Wi-Fi network or over a local hotspot created by the organizer's phone, without any Internet connection and without any server.

  • What is exchanged: the nickname and emblem chosen on each phone, the players and teams of the game (names, colors, emblems) and the game events (throws, corrections, player changes). Player photos are never sent. No phone identifier is sent.
  • Security: the connection is encrypted (TLS) and checked against a fingerprint shown in the QR code; the organizer approves every phone that joins and can disconnect it at any time. The invitation in the QR code expires after 2 minutes.
  • Who receives it: only the phones of that game. Each phone keeps a copy of the game in its own history.

The app declares the Internet permission because Android requires it for any network connection, including on the local network. The app only connects to phones of the game, on private local addresses; it never connects to an Internet server.

5. Permissions

  • Camera (optional): to scan the organizer's QR code. Photos for a player or a share card are taken with your phone's camera app or picked from your gallery.
  • Nearby devices / local network: to create the local hotspot and connect phones during a game played on several phones. Declared as never used to determine location.
  • Notifications (optional): to show the game in progress and, on the organizer's phone, that sharing is active.
  • Network and Wi-Fi state, foreground service: to keep the shared game reachable while the organizer's screen is off.

The app never asks for your location, contacts, microphone or files. You can refuse any permission: the app keeps working on a single phone.

6. Things you choose to send

  • Sharing a moment: images, videos or GIFs are created on your phone and sent only to the app you pick in the Android share sheet. They contain only the information shown on them, without metadata.
  • Backup file: created only when you ask, saved where you choose.
  • Crash report or feedback: sent only if you choose to, by e-mail from your own e-mail app, after you have seen its full content.
  • Voice announcements (off by default): the text to read is handed to the speech engine installed on your phone.

7. Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may process data about installs and app stability under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Yogi Studio only sees the aggregated statistics provided by the Play Console.

8. Children

Molkky Board is intended for teenagers and adults (13 and over) and is not directed to children.

9. Your rights

Because Yogi Studio holds no data about you, your data stays under your control on your phone: you can view, export (Settings › Data › “Back up my data”) and delete it at any time. For any question, write to yogistudiofr@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

10. Changes

If the app starts processing data differently (for example with optional accounts in a future version), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
