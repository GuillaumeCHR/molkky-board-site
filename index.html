Keep score of your Mölkky games, on one phone or several

Molkky Board counts the points, applies the rules (50 exactly, back to 25, out after three misses) and lets you fix any throw at any time. Several phones can follow and enter the same game, directly over Wi-Fi or a local hotspot: no account, no server, no Internet.

No ads, no trackers, no data collected. Free software under the GNU GPL v3: source code.

Coming soon on Google Play. Questions or feedback: yogistudiofr@gmail.com.
